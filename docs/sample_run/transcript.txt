Nuclear energy's unparalleled capacity for continuous, high-density, carbon-free power is not a hyperbolic claim but a scientifically verifiable fact and an operational reality. The so-called 'barriers' of speed, cost, and scalability are not reasons to dismiss nuclear, but rather precisely why it must be prioritized. Nuclear technology, especially with the advent of advanced modular reactors, is intrinsically scalable, with a lifecycle cost that delivers immense value and energy security. The 'speed' of decarbonization is not measured solely by initial construction timelines but by the immediate and enduring dispatchable clean energy output that nuclear plants provide for decades, far outperforming the intermittent nature of other sources. Dismissing nuclear is not a pragmatic solution; it is a concession to perpetual reliance on fossil fuels and a dereliction of our climate duty. Nuclear energy is the singular, indispensable keystone for truly comprehensive, rapid, and resilient decarbonization.
